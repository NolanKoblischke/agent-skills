Day 13

Tue 28 Jul 2026

Spent the morning on the Sector 30 scattered-light problem. Fitted a per-camera background model to the 2 400 lowest-flux pixels of each FFI cutout rather than a single median value, then re-extracted light curves for the 71 targets that fall in the affected CCDs. Residual straylight amplitude drops from ~1 800 e⁻/s to ~240 e⁻/s across the perigee passage, which is finally below the eclipse depths I care about.

Afternoon: re-ran the BLS search on the corrected set and compared recovered periods against the SPOC two-minute targets that overlap my sample (n = 34) as a sanity check. Agreement is good — 31/34 within 0.1%. Started writing the injection-recovery harness so completeness can be quantified properly instead of by hand-waving.

  • —Re-extraction of 71 Sector 30 light curves (3.1 h wall clock, 40 cores)
  • —Cross-match against SPOC 2-min sample, 34 overlapping targets
  • —Skeleton of inject_recover.py — signal injection done, recovery loop not yet
  1. A1
    The scattered-light background is spatially smooth on the scale of a 15 × 15 px cutout, so a single low-order surface per cutout is sufficient.
  2. A2
    SPOC two-minute periods are treated as ground truth for the cross-check.
  3. A3
    Blending from neighbours brighter than T = 16 is negligible after difference imaging.
  • □Finish the recovery loop in inject_recover.py; 10 000 injections across the T–P grid.
  • □Add a 2P/½P check to the BLS post-processing and re-fold the 3 discrepant targets.
  • □Pull Gaia DR3 neighbour counts within 21″ to bound the blending in A3.
  • □Ask about dropping Sector 30 vs. keeping the flagged 9 targets.

Day 12

Mon 27 Jul 2026

First full BLS run over the whole 412-target sample. Period grid 0.1–27 d, uniform in log period, 1 400 phase bins. Total 52 min on 40 cores. Wrote the peak-extraction step (top five peaks per target with their SDE) and a first-pass automated cut at SDE > 8, which keeps 289 targets.

Phase-folded 40 of them by hand to see what the cut is actually letting through. Most look like genuine eclipses; roughly a fifth are clearly folded at twice the true period, and a handful are pulsators that BLS is happy to call a box.
  1. A1
    SDE > 8 is an acceptable detection threshold.
  2. A2
    A 27 d upper period limit is fine because a single sector cannot constrain anything longer.
  • ✓Fix the Sector 30 background before trusting any of these detections.
  • ✓Cross-check against SPOC two-minute targets.
  • □Build the injection-recovery harness — carried to Day 13.

Day 11

Fri 24 Jul 2026

Finished the aperture-selection study. For each target I extracted light curves through nine circular apertures (r = 1.0–3.0 px in 0.25 px steps) and picked the one minimising the point-to-point scatter of the out-of-eclipse flux. Optimum sits at r ≈ 1.75 px for T = 13–14 and r ≈ 1.25 px for T = 14–15, as expected from the sky-noise argument.

Also wrote the quality-flag mask (spacecraft momentum dumps, Earth/Moon crossings) and confirmed against the sector data-release notes that I am removing the right cadences and not more.
  1. A1
    Minimising point-to-point scatter is a fair proxy for maximising eclipse S/N.
  2. A2
    The TESS PSF is constant across a single 15 × 15 px cutout.

  • ✓Decide a provisional detection threshold.
